# Pitch — 4 min + 2 min Q&A

Judges are clinicians. Lead with the patient/clinician problem, not the tech.

| # | Slide | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem | 0:40 | One real story from our medical teammate + one NHS number |
| 2 | Who it's for | 0:20 | User (e.g. ward nurse, surgeon, patient), where in the pathway |
| 3 | Live demo | 1:30 | One happy path, end to end. Have a recorded backup video |
| 4 | Safety & data | 0:50 | Synthetic data, no PII, clinician in the loop, limitations |
| 5 | Impact & next steps | 0:40 | Time/cost saved, how a hospital would pilot it |

## Map to judging criteria
- **Creativity** → slide 1–2: why our angle is new
- **Technical** → slide 3: show the AI actually doing something
- **Impact** → slide 1 + 5: safe and feasible
- **Design & UX** → slide 3: clean, large text, accessible

## Q&A prep (likely questions)
- How would this fit into existing NHS systems (EPR, e.g. EMIS / Cerner)?
- What happens when the AI is wrong?
- What data would you need for a real pilot, and how would you govern it?
- Who is liable / is it a medical device?

## Rehearse
Time it at least twice. Medical teammate opens (problem), builder demos.
